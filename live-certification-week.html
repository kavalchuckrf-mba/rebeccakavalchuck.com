Live Certification Week: bringing a self-paced academy to life

I conceived and led a live, multi-day certification event that turned a self-paced video academy into an instructor-led experience. Both years it ran, it produced the highest monthly certification count of the year.

The challenge

Our newly launched Academy certification program was built on short-form videos and articles. That suits independent, self-paced learners, but not everyone learns that way.

Many learners absorb material better in a live setting, where they can ask questions in the moment and work through content on a defined schedule. Without that structure, they were less likely to start or finish a certification. We needed a way to reach them and build momentum behind the new program, without rebuilding the curriculum from scratch.

The approach

I proposed a live certification event to complement the video series. Instead of writing new curriculum, we translated the existing video scripts into webinar scripts and delivered them over several days in 90–120 minute sessions.

The first year covered two certifications: Compensation Fundamentals and one of our flagship products, whose video scripts I had also written.

A tiered format that builds day by day

Each learner attends only as far as their goal requires

The flagship product course already had beginner, intermediate and advanced tiers. I mirrored that structure across three days, with each day building on the one before. Exam links went out at the end of the matching day, so learners could certify while the material was fresh.

Execution

Working within the tools we had, then scaling once the model was proven.

2024 · Zoom

Working around platform limits

Zoom couldn't handle registration for a multi-day, multi-level event. I built a simple Asana intake form that asked which certification level each registrant wanted, then imported registrations into the right webinar days so everyone got invites for exactly the sessions they needed.

2025 · Goldcast

Scaling with the right tools

We moved to Goldcast, which supports multi-day events and sessions natively. Learners chose their own sessions and the manual registration work went away. We kept all existing content and added certifications for two more products using the same tiered method.

Compensation Fundamentals

Enabling subject matter experts

Key takeaways

Meet learners where they are.

Adding a live option to self-paced content reached a different audience and multiplied certifications.

Reuse before you rebuild.

Adapting existing video scripts delivered a new format without new curriculum.

Let structure do the work.

A tiered, cumulative schedule let each learner commit only the time their goal required.

Solve for today's tools, then scale.

A simple Asana workaround launched the event on Zoom; Goldcast removed the manual work once it was proven.

Build a repeatable model.

The same method extended to two more product certifications in year two.
